Case Studies

Market Entry Strategy — Vascudex / SONOVEIN Health Tech
↓
Led the full market entry project for SONOVEIN, a non-invasive HIFU-based treatment for varicose veins, in collaboration with Vall d'Hebron University Hospital (Barcelona, 2025). Identified a high-value prospect segment totalling €70M in potential pipeline value. Conducted TAM/SAM/SOM analysis across the Spanish cardiovascular market (321,500 patients / €1.2B–€1.4B TAM). Profiled 15+ local competitors and mapped 3 potential collaboration partners. Defined 3 data-driven target personas based on NHS and INE data. Delivered an integrated marketing guide covering market segmentation, competitive forces, a 3-tier value-based pricing model (€3,500–€7,000), and a full multichannel GTM workflow.
€70M pipeline identified 15+ competitors mapped 3 target personas defined Tools: Clay · Apollo · Salesforce · HubSpot
Durability Strategy & Consumer Research — Camper Consumer Brand
↓
Led a cross-functional project for Camper in collaboration with ESADE, focused on product durability strategy, consumer research, and sustainability communications (Barcelona, 2024–2025). Designed and launched a bilingual customer survey distributed to 57,445 members of Camper's Walking Society loyalty program. Segmented audience by CRM data to maximise response quality. Additional deliverables: ESG labelling audit averting €273K+ in potential fines, competitor benchmark covering 20 brands, and stakeholder alignment across 6 expert interviews.
